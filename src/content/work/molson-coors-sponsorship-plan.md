## The brief

Plan sponsorships that put Molson Coors brands in front of Wisconsin adults at local events they already love, with a line-item budget and a clear way to measure whether it worked.

## The plan

Two partnerships, both deeply Wisconsin:

- **Wisconsin State Fair racing pigs ($654K).** Four racing pigs, each sponsored by a brand: Miller Lite, Coors Light, Leinenkugel's Summer Shandy and Coors Edge non-alcoholic. Fans join a prize-based game, with branded koozie and T-shirt giveaways.
- **A Coors Light Deck with the Milwaukee Milkmen at Franklin Field ($346K).** A branded deck, a #CoorsLightDeck social campaign, free merchandise, private events and meet-and-greets.

## Audience

Adults 21 to 40 and health-conscious consumers, with a firm rule of no marketing to minors. Coors Edge gave the plan a non-alcoholic option for the health-conscious segment.

## Measurement

Line-item budgets for both partnerships, and KPIs including attendance, drinks sold, tickets sold and social tags, with fan-experience surveys after each event.

## Results

This was a class plan, so there are no live results. What it delivered was a complete, itemized $1.0M plan with KPIs for each partnership, and a document I've since reviewed with fresh eyes. The changes are in the Remaster below.
