## The brief

Peyton Pours is a private bartending business serving showers, dinners and private events in the Lake Country area. I joined in August 2026 to support events and marketing as the brand launched.

## What I did

- **Events, end to end.** Supported three private events, covering setup, bar service and the guest experience.
- **Menus.** Co-designed event drink menus in Figma. Two are tucked in below as [inserts](#inserts).
- **Brand.** Supported brand development for the new business, alongside Peyton, who leads the brand.
- **Launch marketing.** Contributed marketing ideas and handled marketing tasks during the launch.
