## The brief

Tabal is a bean-to-bar chocolate maker in Wauwatosa. Our team of three built a complete PR campaign to raise its profile with Milwaukee-area audiences.

## Research

We started with a situation statement, a SWOT analysis and a competitive analysis, then built target-market profiles from demographic and psychographic segmentation, using Claritas PRIZM, VALS and U.S. Census data.

## The campaign

- A **media kit** for local press.
- An **email pitch** to a Milwaukee TV morning show.
- A **radio ad script**.
- **Instagram post copy**.
- A **QR-code flyer and handout**, built to turn in-person interest into email sign-ups.

## Measurement

A campaign timeline and evaluation KPIs covering sales, social engagement, email sign-ups and website traffic.

## Results

A class campaign, so no live numbers. The deliverables are real and complete, and looking back, a few claims and goals needed work. That's the Remaster below.

Fact check: [Askinosie Chocolate](https://www.askinosie.com), Springfield, MO.
